  Instructions
    
    
  Fitness planner enables you to keep a track of your exercise routine on a monthly and a weekly basis. A calendar notes sheet of the current month is provided along with the exercise logs.
    
  How to create a Fitness Plan? 
  Open the default file. 
  Choose the exercises that you would be doing for the week and create goals. 
  Track the activities on a weekly basis as you perform them. 
  Weekly and monthly summary are automatically summarised for you.  
  Create notes of your fitness plan in the memo section. Please save files like "FitnessAprPhone". 
    
  How to prevent re-entering the repetitive information again in the Fitness Planner? 
  Enter the repetitive details in the default file. Save as Plan1. Go to List Files. Use Plan1 every time you wish to prevent entering the information again.  
    
  How to save files to Dropbox? 
  Click on Login to Dropbox. Enter the login credentials.  
  Login to Dropbox" button should change to "Logout from Dropbox" on successful login. 
  Click on "Save Files to Dropbox". Please note that the number of files should be more than 1. Select the files by using checkmarks and click on "Save to Dropbox". 
  The selected files get saved to Dropbox successfully. Click on "List Dropbox Files" to view them. 
    
  How to retrieve files from Dropbox? 
  Click on "List Dropbox Files". 
  Click on "Move to iPad". 
  Select files by using checkmarks and click on "Save To Device". 
  The selected files get moved to iPad successfully. Click on "List Files" to view them.
