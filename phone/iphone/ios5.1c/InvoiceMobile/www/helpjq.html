 Instructions
   
   
 Invoice Suite App is a one stop solution for small and large business owners looking for an invoicing app used in common form in both domestic and international trade. 
   
 How to create an Invoice? 
 Open the default file. 
 Choose the suitable template. 
 Add the details about you and your clients along with specifics of the order, shipment details and tax rates. 
 Taxes and totals are automatically calculated.  
 Add your invoice items and Invoice Suite does the rest.  
   
 How to prevent re-entering the company information again in the Invoice? 
 Enter the company name and contact details in the default file. Save as Invoice1. Go to List Files. Use Invoice1 every time you wish to prevent entering the information again.  
   
 How to use Type3 Invoice? 
 Click on Type3 sheet. Enter the "Bill To" and "From" information.  
 Enter the details in "Detail1" or "Detail2" sheets or both.  
 Please note that if you are using both these sheets then the description field should be entered alternatively.
