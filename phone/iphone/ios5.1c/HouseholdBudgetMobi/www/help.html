Instructions 
     
     
  Invoice Suite App is a one stop solution for small and large business owners looking for an invoicing app used in common form in both domestic and international trade.  A feature packed app that enables you to generate, modify, organize and export billing information to your customers and partners. The intuitive interface allows you to choose from suitable templates and produce invoices.  
     
  How to create an Invoice?  
  Open the default file.  
  Choose the suitable template.  
  Add the details about you and your clients along with specifics of the order, shipment details and tax rates.  
  Taxes and totals are automatically calculated for you.   
  Add your invoice items and Invoice Suite does the rest.   
     
  How to prevent re-entering the company information again in the Invoice?  
  Enter the company name and contact details in the default file. Save as Invoice1. Go to List Files. Use Invoice1 every time you wish to prevent entering the information again.   
     
  How to save files to Dropbox?  
  Click on Login to Dropbox. Enter the login credentials.   
  "Login to Dropbox" button should change to "Logout from Dropbox" on successful login.  
  Click on "Save Files to Dropbox". Please note that the number of files should be more than 1. Select the files by using checkmarks and click on "Save to Dropbox".  
  The selected files get saved to Dropbox successfully. Click on "List Dropbox Files" to view them.  
     
  How to retrieve files from Dropbox?  
  Click on "List Dropbox Files".  
  Click on "Move to iPad".  
  Select files by using checkmarks and click on "Save To Device".  
  The selected files get moved to iPad successfully. Click on "List Files" to view them.
