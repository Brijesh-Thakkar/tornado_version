App Description

If you are just getting started managing your finances or have a new services business and need a quick solution for developing a business budget comprising of your income, expenses and their sources, Services Business Budget is the best app for you. You can separate income and business expenses into categories that closely match those used in an income statement. The categories are fairly comprehensive, but it is also easy to modify the categories.

Features

  • Add and edit budgeted and actual entries for operating and non-operating income.
  • Add and edit budgeted and actual entries for operating and recurring expenses under various sub-categories.
  • Visualize the business income, expenses and savings through graphs and charts.
  • Get a pie chart analysis showing the distribution of business expenses.
  • Get a comparative account of the actual and budgeted value of business income and expenses.
  • Set goals for business income and get an account on weekly and monthly basis.
