App Description

If you're a small business owner or a freelancer, who wants to send invoices while you're on the job, Invoice Suite is an easy to use app for you. Invoice Suite allows you to manage and send estimates and invoices on the go. The intuitive interface allows you to produce invoices in just a minute wherever you may be. Further, the app provides you with a range of professional invoice styles to choose from. Simply tab on the style and choose the one you like.

Features

  • Range of professional invoice styles to choose from. Simply tab on the style and choose the one you like
  • Automatic calculation of taxes and totals
  • E-mail, to send your invoice to clients instantly and get paid faster
  • Locally save the invoice as a file, and re-use it with minimal changes
  • Intuitive interface
