App Description

If you are just getting started managing your financial accounts or have a new business and need a quick solution for tracking expenses and account transactions, Account Balance is the best app for you. You can record all kinds of transactions along with their details like date, description, payee information, amount to be debited or credited, and develop an aggregate balance.

Features

  • Record all kinds of transactions along with their details like date, description, payee information, amount to be debited or credited, and develop an aggregate balance.
  • Save and re-use the Account Balance. Keep a track of the financial progress over time.
  • Share the transaction details from and to the account with your business partners via email.
  • Locally save, email the Account Balance transaction details
